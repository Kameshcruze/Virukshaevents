Frequently Asked Questions

Do you manage destination weddings?

Yes. Viruksha Events plans and executes destination weddings across South India including Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana.

Which districts do you serve in Tamil Nadu?

We serve Coimbatore, Erode, Tirupur, Karur, Salem, Namakkal, Tiruchy, Madurai, Palani and other districts across Tamil Nadu.

Do you provide event management outside Tamil Nadu?

Yes. We also manage events in Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana, offering complete wedding and corporate solutions.

Do you provide corporate event management?

Yes. We organize corporate events including product launches, showroom openings, conferences, and team celebrations across South India.

Do you handle traditional family functions?

Absolutely. We specialize in weddings, puberty functions, baby showers, cradle ceremonies, and cultural events with authentic decor.

Can you customize themes and decorations?

Yes. We provide theme weddings, pathway decorations, balloon setups, garlands, and personalized decor concepts tailored to your vision.
